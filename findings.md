# N1: the published findings being re-tested

**Verification status (23 Nov 2026, provisional; AI-checked, needs a person's check).** Thompson et al. 2017 was checked word for word against the open-access full text (Europe PMC JATS XML of PMC6192678, retrieved 23 Nov 2026). Only four of the ten rules (F1, F2, F3, F7) are claims made by Thompson et al. 2017; F4–F6 and F8–F10 come from other papers and were checked against those papers' abstracts (Europe PMC records, retrieved 23 Nov 2026). Where only the abstract was available this is stated. Paragraph numbers below count paragraphs within the named section of the PMC full text.

> **Framing correction.** The project has been described as "re-testing ten biogeography rules from Thompson 2017". That is not accurate: Thompson 2017 is the source of F1–F3 and F7, mentions the saline/non-saline split behind F4, and reports a latitude result that *contradicts* F5. The manuscript says "ten widely cited rules, four of them from the EMP flagship paper".

## Findings whose test no longer matches the published claim (summary)

1. **F7** — proxy metric (prevalence–richness correlation), not NODF; different taxonomic level. Reword as a corollary test.
2. **F4** — moderate pH/temperature splits and Jaccard distance vs L&K's extremes and UniFrac. Reword the verdict.
3. **F10** — source may concern Acidobacteria only (person to confirm); rule contested.
4. **F2 (MicrobeAtlas only)** — sample set includes host-associated samples; the claim concerns non-host-associated ones.
5. **F1/F2** — estimator binning differs from the paper (pre-specified); not a mismatch of the claim.
