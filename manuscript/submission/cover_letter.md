Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, “Uneven sampling hides and creates rules of microbial biogeography,” for publication as [article type] in [journal].

We examine how study and geographic sampling imbalance affects ten published microbial biogeography rules using Earth Microbiome Project and MicrobeAtlas data. The manuscript compares sample-, study-, country- and grid-weighted estimates and evaluates prediction in held-out countries. It reports that some conclusions vary with weighting, including the relationship between soil Acidobacteria and pH.

We believe the study will interest readers concerned with microbial ecology, geographic representation and the interpretation of large-scale observational data. The manuscript also discusses the limits of the proxy measures, data coverage and transfer tests.
